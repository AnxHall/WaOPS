# Domain Boundaries

## Identity
Usuários, autenticação, sessões, MFA futuro.

## Tenancy
Tenant, organization/subtenant, membership e escopo.

## RBAC
Roles, permissions e authorization service.

## Commercial Access
Plans, products, modules, entitlements, quotas, usage.

## Resource Catalog
Hosts, agents, containers, services, databases, endpoints, repos.

## Observability
Monitors, checks, metrics metadata e rules.

## Incident
Events, incidents, acknowledgements, timeline e correlation.

## Support
Tickets, queues, SLA, comments, attachments.

## Knowledge
Documents, versions, runbooks, changes.

## Integrations
Notification providers, payment providers, GitHub, WAHA, AI providers.

## Regra
Um domínio não acessa tabelas de outro diretamente quando houver invariant relevante; usar serviço/contrato.
